Observer correction found during review¶

The earlier kinds 25–27 use BLTCPT=$11000 and BLTDPT=$10000. In line mode, later D writes follow the updated C pointer. Their OR scan of $10000–$107fe and words $10000/$10050/$100a0 therefore observe the initial D region, not all later line writes. Those checked words match across the captures, but this does not establish that every generated line pixel is zero or that all later destination words agree. The kind-28 observer must scan both regions and sample $11050/$110a0 before flag semantics are inferred.

Shift and history controls¶

Kind 33 repeats the zero-B, $ca, one/two-pixel test across all 16 A and B shifts, with and without B DMA (1,024 rows). A second hardware run of the identical ROM differs in only two BZERO observations; beams and all sampled memory agree. One-pixel cases keep BZERO set. Two-pixel cases produce an irregular shift-dependent pattern that the universal $ffff intermediate-B hypothesis cannot explain. These are effective A & B masks, not independently measured B shifter words. The earlier A/C-stage experiment only establishes its rule for the particular A shifts tested.

Kind 34 adds four preconditions to a 1,024-row subset: no priming, a normal B-data register write, and normal two-word B+D copies using zero and $ffff data. All four produce nearly the same line BZERO patterns. Normal shifter history alone is therefore insufficient to explain the residual. Kind 34 source ID is 07aa9a9c91bef61cf337e3b4bbf2c23d807504b8a77b0c3664acf86bcee385b8; hardware packet SHA-256 is c55347b0ae74d0d12b57f6169f874c937ceee2572f434640e71df5218a56bb37.
